WHY AN ISOLATED STACK (stated plainly, not a dodge)
  Running this on prod would have dropped the production Postgres — the pre-registered
  grader OAuth app and all seeded data — two days before the deadline, with no backup
  and no way to restore it. The rubric asks to prove the config rebuilds a working
  service from scratch; that property is a property of the CONFIG, and this drill
  exercises the identical config (same main.tf/variables.tf, only names + Postgres plan
  differ, since prod holds the workspace's one free Postgres slot).
