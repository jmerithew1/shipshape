ROOT CAUSE of 5 of the 6 hard failures: the specs hardcoded the `Meta` (Cmd)
modifier for editor shortcuts and select-all. Off macOS, ProseMirror binds
those to `Mod` = Ctrl, and `Meta+A` isn't even select-all — so the shortcut
never fired and the assertion failed. Fixed by switching to Playwright's
platform-aware `ControlOrMeta` key (Ctrl on Win/Linux, Cmd on Mac), matching
the app's `Mod` binding on every platform.
